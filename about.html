Our story

Started with one squeegee and a standard.

Reggie's was founded after years working in bars and restaurants — where you learn fast that service either meets a standard or it doesn't. There's no halfway when someone's paying for an experience, and that mindset never left.

We wanted to bring that same level of care to home services, an industry that's too often let people down with no-shows, rushed jobs and no communication. So Reggie's set out to do the opposite: turn up when we say we will, do the job properly, and treat every home like it's the only booking we've got that day.

It's why every member of our team has previous hospitality experience. They already know what it means to look after people properly — we just point that same energy at your windows, gutters and solar panels.
