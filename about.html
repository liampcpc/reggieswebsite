Our story

Started with one squeegee and a standard.

Reggie's was founded after years working in premium hotel facilities management — where the detail standard is uncompromising and every window, every morning, has to be perfect.

He brought that standard home. What started as a word-of-mouth residential business in Melbourne's inner north has grown into a full-service cleaning operation covering windows, gutters, and solar panels across greater Melbourne.

Every client gets the same experience: on time, thorough, respectful of your home, and genuinely proud of the result.
