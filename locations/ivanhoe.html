About this area

Beautiful trees. Blocked gutters.

Famous for its undulating hills, massive native trees, and stunning mid-century architecture, the Ivanhoe area is beautiful but demanding on your roof. Overhanging branches in Eaglemont and the Yarra parklands means gutters fill quickly, and windows need regular washing to let that natural light in.

We specialise in the unique demands of the north east. Whether you need a deep gutter flush before the storm season in Rosanna, or streak-free glass for a panoramic view in Alphington, Reggie's delivers consistent, premium results.
