About this area

Village shops, elevated streets and serious tree cover.

From the small strip of shops around Eaglemont Village — the butcher, the cellars, the local cafes — to the elevated, tree-lined streets climbing away from the station, Eaglemont is one of the north east's quietest and greenest pockets, with a genuine mix of interwar homes and architect-designed mid-century builds tucked among the hills.

Reggie's knows Eaglemont well. From clearing box gutters under mature native trees to keeping a mid-century home's picture windows streak-free, we bring the same hospitality-grade care to every house, whatever its era.
