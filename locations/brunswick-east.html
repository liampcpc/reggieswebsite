About this area

Workers' cottages meet a creative, creek-side community.

From the narrow single-fronted cottages off Lygon Street to the community gardens and renewable-energy culture around CERES on the Merri Creek, Brunswick East has one of the most tight-knit, environmentally-minded communities in Melbourne's inner north — heritage workers' cottages sitting alongside sharp modern townhouse infill.

Reggie's knows Brunswick East well. Built for Brunswick East's housing mix

Every era, treated properly.

Brunswick East isn't architecturally uniform, and we don't treat it like it is.

Victorian workers' cottages
Narrow single-fronted terraces with original timber-framed windows and tight eaves not built for modern downpipe loads. We access these roof lines safely without damaging heritage frames.
Townhouse infill & renovations
Large glass frontages squeezed onto older blocks. A streak-free finish on big panes and the tracks that collect grit from daily use — treated like a hotel lobby window, every time.
Solar households
Brunswick East's environmentally-minded community has driven strong rooftop solar uptake. Our pure, deionised water-fed pole system restores output without chemicals, abrasives, or touching your warranty.
