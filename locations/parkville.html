About this area

Leafy streets between Royal Park and the university precinct.

Home to the Royal Melbourne Zoo, Royal Park's open grasslands and a cluster of major hospitals and university faculties, Parkville is one of Melbourne's greenest inner suburbs — a mix of grand period homes, post-war houses and modern medical-precinct apartments, all sitting close to some of the city's largest parkland.

Reggie's treats a Parkville job the same as any other: careful, photo-documented, and finished properly the first time. Whether it's a heritage home near the zoo or an apartment overlooking the park, we bring the same hospitality-grade standard.
