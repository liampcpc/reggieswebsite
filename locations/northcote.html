About this area

A leafy, creative pocket of the inner north.

From the tram-and-café strip along High Street to the quieter streets running down toward the Merri Creek, Northcote is one of Melbourne's most-loved inner-north suburbs — heritage weatherboards and Edwardian terraces sitting alongside thoughtful renovations, all under one of the leafiest tree canopies in the area.

Reggie's knows Northcote well. From navigating original timber window frames in a Clarke Street cottage to keeping a Westgarth renovation's glass extension streak-free, we bring the same hospitality-grade care to every home, whatever era it was built in. We're already on your street too — Reggie's runs regular rounds through Brunswick, Coburg and Clifton Hill, all bordering Northcote.

Built for Northcote's housing mix

Every era, treated properly.

Northcote isn't architecturally uniform, and we don't treat it like it is.

Heritage weatherboards & Edwardian terraces
Original timber-framed windows, narrow eaves, and valley or box gutters not built for modern downpipe loads. We access these roof lines safely and clean original timber frames without stripping decades-old paint.
Renovations & glass extensions
Bifold doors, floor-to-ceiling glass, skylights. A streak-free finish on large panes and the tracks that collect grit from daily use — treated like a hotel lobby window, every time.
Solar households
Darebin has one of the highest rates of residential solar uptake in inner Melbourne. Our pure, deionised water-fed pole system restores output without chemicals, abrasives, or touching your warranty.
  • Downpipes blocked with jacaranda and plane-tree debris, especially near All Nations Park
  • Valley gutters on Edwardian roof lines silting up faster than standard box gutters
  • Solar arrays showing a visible dust and pollen film after just one season
  • Window frames on rear extensions collecting street dust and garden pollen regular rain won't clear
