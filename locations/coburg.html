About this area

A working-class heart that's still growing.

Coburg has one of the most diverse streetscapes in Melbourne's inner north — Californian bungalows and post-war brick veneers along the quieter side streets, the multicultural food strip running down Sydney Road, and the Pentridge Village redevelopment turning the old prison grounds into apartments and townhouses.

Reggie's knows Coburg's mix well. From clearing gum-tree debris off an established bungalow's gutters near Coburg Lake to keeping the floor-to-ceiling glass in a new Pentridge apartment streak-free, we bring the same hospitality-grade care whatever the build. We're already on your street too — Reggie's runs regular rounds through Brunswick and Pascoe Vale, both bordering Coburg.
