We see it on almost every job: gutters that look fine from the ground and are, in reality, holding six months of decomposing leaf mulch that's already started rotting the fascia board underneath.

None of this is unique to any one street — it's a function of the suburb's tree density and housing age, which is exactly why a generic once-a-year clean from a big franchise often isn't enough here.

### Frequently asked questions — Northcote

All of it — from the High Street shops down to the Merri Creek side, and just as easily into Thornbury or Westgarth if that's more accurate to where you are.

With the tree canopy here, we generally recommend twice a year — once after the big autumn leaf drop, and again before summer storm season. Homes right on a treed street or backing onto the creek sometimes need a mid-cycle check.

**Will cleaning our solar panels void the manufacturer's warranty?**
No. We use a deionised, pure-water system with soft brushes only — no detergents, no pressure washing, no abrasives. This is the maintenance method manufacturers actually recommend.

Yes. We assess roof pitch and gutter type on every quote and use the right access method (ladder, roof anchor points, or extension pole systems) rather than defaulting to one approach for every home.

Yes — we clean timber frames by hand with appropriate products, not harsh chemicals that strip old paintwork, and we're careful around original sash mechanisms.

**Do you offer a discount if we book gutters, windows and solar together?**
Yes — most Northcote clients bundle two or three services in the same visit, and we offer a discounted combined rate rather than pricing each as a separate call-out.

**How quickly can you get to us once we book?**
